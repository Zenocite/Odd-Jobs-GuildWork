# Odd Jobs

Tester build 1.9.0 for OpenMW 0.51. You need Tamriel Data and Tamriel Rebuilt aswell as Ui Toolkit Add the folder as a data directory and enable OddJobs.esp, then OddJobs.omwscripts.

Papers are pinned to the wall beside the door. Walk outside a tavern, a guild hall, a Legion fort, an East Empire Company office, or a village inn and read what is posted. Take the job, do it, and come back to that notice to be paid. You can hold three jobs at once, and a fourth after you have finished fifteen. Until you have finished five, a board only posts a couple of jobs. The contract log key is unbound until you set it under Options, Scripts, Odd Jobs.

Gold is about 50 to 120 for easy work, 130 to 300 for normal, 350 to 650 for dangerous, and 700 to 1150 for elite. Goods or a lesson can come with it. Dangerous work has a red seal. Elite work has a black seal.

## On the notices

- Clear a nest that has moved into a nearby place.
- A bounty on a leader and their people. Bring back the signet.
- Recover something lost in a cave, mine, ruin, shrine, stronghold, wreck, or tomb.
- Goods wanted. Guilds, the Temple, smiths, and traders ask for things they need. Hand them in at the notices.
- A collector wants one particular piece or book.
- Someone lost a ledger out in the street, and the notice says where they went that day.
- Gossip and town news, which are not jobs.
- Now and then a single notice is pinned to a post, a stall, or a house wall somewhere else in town.

A place you cleared can be posted again a week or more later. A champion you let go can come back.

## Out on the road

These are posted on the same boards. The work is outside, not in a dungeon.

- A broken cart by a road. Bring back the strongbox.
- A bandit camp by a road. Bring back the leader's signet.
- A beast taking herds near a road.
- A pack guar that wandered off. Walk it home, or bring back its pack.
- A crate left on a beach for smugglers.
- Shipwreck debris, with the ship's ledger somewhere in it.
- Fresh ingredients to pick in a named region.
- At a Legion fort: a patrol was hit on the road. Bring back its supply crate.
- At a Legion fort: a tax chest went missing. Bring it back with the seal unbroken.
- Someone left town and never arrived.
- Guar to walk out to a camp on the road. Paid for each one delivered.
- Diggers at a tomb door after dark.
- A Dwemer sphere or spider out on the road.
- An atronach loose near a Telvanni tower.
- Cultists at a ring of candles after dark.
- A pirate camp on a beach.
- A named pirate captain. Bring back the hat he wears.

## What can happen on a job

- A leader may give up and offer you gold.
- A note with a lost piece may say it belongs to someone in another town.
- Someone else may have taken the same job.
- A nest you were sent to clear may already be dead, and something tougher may still be inside.
- A leader may carry a letter naming another hideout. Bring it back and a follow-up gets posted.

## Where to look

**Vvardenfell.** Seyda Neen at Arrille's Tradehouse. Balmora at the Eight Plates, the Council Club, the South Wall Cornerclub, the Fighters Guild, and the Mages Guild. Pelagiad at the Halfway Tavern and Fort Pelagiad. Caldera at Shenk's Shovel and the Mages Guild. Ald-ruhn at the Rat in the Pot, the Ald Skar Inn, and both guilds. Vivec in the Foreign Quarter. Ebonheart at the Six Fishes, Hawkmoth Legion Garrison, and the East Empire Company Hall. Suran at the Suran Tradehouse. Sadrith Mora at the Gateway Inn, Dirty Muriel's Cornerclub, and Wolverine Hall. Gnisis at Madach Tradehouse and Fort Darius.

**Legion forts.** Moonmoth, Buckmoth, Fort Frostmoth (Bloodmoon), Fort Ancylis, Windmoth, Fort Umbermoth, Firemoth, and Septim's Gate Pass.

**Tamriel Rebuilt cities.** Old Ebonheart, Firewatch, and Narsis have a tavern board, both guilds, and the forts that belong to them. Necrom has tavern boards. Almas Thirr has taverns and both guilds. East Empire Company orders are also posted at the company office in Ebonheart, Old Ebonheart, Firewatch, and Narsis.

**Village inns on Vvardenfell.** Gnaar Mok, Khuul, Maar Gan, Molag Mar, Tel Branora, Vos, Hla Oad, Dagon Fel, Tel Mora, and Tel Aruhn. These boards are smaller: a couple of jobs, a small order, and a bit of gossip.

**Village inns and roadside taverns on the mainland.** Aimrah, Akamora, Ald Marak, Ald Verya, Andar Mok, Bal Foyen, Bodrum, Bosmora, Crystal Scissure, Enamor Dayn, Gah Sadrith, Hlan Oek, Hlerynhul, Idathren, Llothanis, Marog, Nan Iban, Nivalis, Othmura, Othrenis, Port Telvannis, Roa Dyr, Sailen, Shipal-Sharai, Tel Gilan, Teyn, Uman, the Hunted Hound Inn, Maar-Bani Crossing, Mavandes Caravanserai, Supai Caravanserai, and the Grey Lodge. Ilanaam and Andrethis need the Othreleth Woods plugin. Village boards can be turned off in the script settings.
